How the ranking works

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × revenue quality

  • TVL × category multiple. Each category earns the median market-cap-per-TVL of its protocols that already trade, shrunk toward the overall median 0.17 and bounded 0.10–1.00. A protocol's size is always the base.
  • Growth. Seven-day TVL change, clamped −60% to +120%, at half weight.
  • Maturity. 0.60 plus up to 0.40 for up to three years running with no token.
  • Revenue quality (0.50–2.00×). Annualized protocol revenue per dollar of TVL, against the category median. Revenue is DefiLlama daily revenue, 30-day and 90-day windows ending 14 Nov 2026, the snapshot's own date. Where no revenue is recorded the multiplier is exactly 1.00 — missing data is never punished.
  • Cross-check. Every detail view also shows the revenue-anchored value: annualized revenue × the category's P/S multiple, so you can see where the two lenses agree and where they part.
  • What revenue cannot do. A protocol with no token has no market cap, no FDV and no unlock schedule to measure — the implied value is the FDV estimate. Audit counts are shown as a flag, never as a score.
